I spent almost 10 years in Cisco voice.

Certs. Courses. Labs. Late nights.
Job posts that asked for “everything.”
Training that taught product screens…
but not the quiet things that actually save a call at 2 a.m.

And for a long time, I carried a quiet gap:

I didn’t need another badge.
I needed mentorship.
I needed multi-vendor clarity.
I needed someone to say:

“Here’s the path.
Here’s the evidence.
Here’s how you prove it —
not just how you configure it.”

Because real enterprise voice isn’t one logo.

It’s CUCM and CUBE… and also Teams, Webex, Zoom, Contact Center platforms, SBCs, carriers, certificates, QoS, dual-run migrations, recording, emergency calling, identity, and the human on the other end of the line.

Access was uneven.
Jobs wanted “senior” before anyone offered a map.
Training was expensive, fragmented, and often single-vendor.
I felt that. Deeply.

So I built something I wish I had years ago —

a free UC / Contact Center university pack
I can share with anyone who needs it.

No hype.
No gatekeeping.
Just structured learning:
call path thinking, failure labs, multi-vendor braid, migrations, evidence habits (Leg · ID · Counter · Capture), and room to grow into AI + voice futures.

If it helps even one person feel less alone in this craft —
the late nights were worth it.

I’m happy to share.
I’m happy to contribute more.
If you’re early, mid, or 10 years in and still hungry for real understanding — this is for you.

Open. Learn. Share freely.
If it helps you, pass it to one more engineer.
