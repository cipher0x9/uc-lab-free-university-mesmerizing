I spent almost 10 years in Cisco voice. Certs. Courses. Labs. Late nights. Job posts that asked for “everything.” Training that taught product screens… but not the quiet things that actually save a call at 2 a.m. And for a long time, I carried a quiet gap:
I didn’t need another badge. I needed mentorship. I needed multi-vendor clarity. I needed a map — not more noise.
Because real enterprise voice isn’t one logo. It’s Cisco… and also Teams, Webex, Zoom, contact center platforms, SBCs, carriers, certificates, QoS, dual-run migrations, recording, emergency calling, identity — and the human on the other end of the line. Access was uneven. Jobs wanted “senior” before anyone offered a path. Training was expensive, fragmented, and often single-vendor. I felt that. Deeply. So I built something I wish I had years ago — a free UC / Contact Center learning pack I’m happy to share. No hype. No gatekeeping. Just structured understanding: call paths, failure labs, multi-vendor thinking, migrations, and evidence habits that hold up when production is on fire. If it helps even one person feel less alone in this craft — the late nights were worth it. I’m happy to share. I’m happy to contribute more. If you’re early, mid, or 10 years in and still hungry for real understanding — this is for you. Open. Learn. Share freely. If it helps you, pass it to one more engineer. 🌿 UC Lab Free Share (Link in first comment)
