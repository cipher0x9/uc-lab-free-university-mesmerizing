SIPREC (RFC 7866) lets a session recording client send a copy of a call to a session recording server, with metadata in RFC 7865. A Cisco built-in bridge forks from the phone. A SPAN port copies frames on a switch. This page describes the mechanisms. It is not legal advice about who may be recorded.

    Failure tree

    Recording session starts and the file is silent

    Checks. SDP direction on the SIPREC INVITE, and whether RTP actually reaches the SRS.

    Likely cause. The recording dialog is up and the media fork points at the wrong address.

    Fix. Compare the SRS SDP with the packets that arrive.

    Only one party is recorded

    Checks. Is the fork after the mix, or two separate streams?

    Likely cause. The SRC sent one direction.

    Fix. Ask for both directions in the recording session. Confirm in the capture.

    SPAN capture misses the call

    Checks. Which switch port was mirrored.

    Likely cause. Media was anchored somewhere else, so the mirrored port never saw RTP.

    Fix. Mirror the port that actually carries RTP, or use SIPREC from that anchor.
