Move one failure domain at a time. A TDM trunk becomes a SIP trunk. A CUCM site can keep its phones while PSTN shifts to a Webex Calling Local Gateway, or the phones can move later. A contact center can send one queue to CCaaS before the rest. Skype for Business telephony moves to Teams Phone by user, not by unplugging the estate. This page is a cutover sequence, not a vendor quote.

    Pilot works and the second site has no inbound

    Checks. Where the carrier points the DID.

    Likely cause. The numbers were forwarded in the PBX and never moved at the carrier.

    Fix. Move the numbers at the carrier when you move the site. Keep one test DID.

    911 was not in the pilot

    Checks. A 911 test from a pilot phone, in the lab window the carrier allows.

    Likely cause. The new path has no location records.

    Fix. Do not expand the pilot until 911 has a dispatchable location.

    Rollback has no trunk

    Checks. The old trunk is still in service and the route can prefer it.

    Likely cause. The old trunk was turned down on day one.

    Fix. Leave the old path until the new path has survived a business day.
