Read the ladder before you read a codec. Wireshark's Telephony VoIP Calls window builds that ladder from a capture, and RTP Stream Analysis shows loss and jitter. sngrep shows SIP on a host in a terminal. Cisco RTMT shows cluster counters. On CUBE, debug ccsip messages prints SIP, and show sip-ua status shows the user agent. Example addresses only: 192.0.2.10, 198.51.100.10, 203.0.113.20.

    Failure tree

    The ladder shows 486 and the ticket says one-way audio

    Checks. There is no 200 OK and no RTP.

    Likely cause. The call was busy. Audio was never negotiated.

    Fix. Close the audio theory. Find out why the destination sent 486.

    Wireshark shows RTP and sngrep shows nothing

    Checks. Which host you ran sngrep on.

    Likely cause. sngrep sees SIP on that host. Media-only hops will not appear.

    Fix. Capture on the device that has both, or use Wireshark for RTP.

    debug ccsip messages is empty

    Checks. Are you on the CUBE that receives the call, and is the terminal monitor on?

    Likely cause. The call is on a different router, or logging is not displayed.

    Fix. Confirm the hop with show sip-ua status, then debug on that box, then turn the debug off.
