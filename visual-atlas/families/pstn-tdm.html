Circuit voice: a DS0 is 64 kbit/s. North American T1 carries 24 channels at 1.544 Mbit/s. E1 carries 32 timeslots at 2.048 Mbit/s. PRI is 23B+D on T1 and 30B+D on E1. Call setup on the D channel is ITU-T Q.931. Between exchanges, ISUP runs over SS7, and SIGTRAN carries MTP3 signaling over IP with M3UA and SCTP.

      Failure tree

      PRI stays down after a carrier test

      Checks. Layer 1 framing and line code, then Q.921 on the D channel (SABME / UA), then Q.931.

      Likely cause. Framing mismatch (ESF vs SF, or B8ZS vs AMI) or the D channel is not in multiple-frame established state.

      Fix. Match the carrier's framing and line code. Confirm Q.921 is up before debugging Q.931 SETUP.

      Calls ring but have no talk path

      Checks. Which B channel was assigned in the CONNECT, and whether both sides use the same channel numbering.

      Likely cause. One side is counting B channels from a different timeslot map (NFAS or a shifted E1 map).

      Fix. Compare the channel ID information element with the trunk timeslot map.

      ISUP call releases at once

      Checks. REL cause code.

      Likely cause. Unallocated number, congestion, or a missing circuit group.

      Fix. Read the cause. Do not change the dial plan until the cause matches the symptom.
