STIR (RFC 8224) puts a PASSporT (RFC 8225) in the SIP Identity header, signed with a certificate (RFC 8226). SHAKEN is the profile in RFC 8588 and ATIS-1000074. Attestation A means the provider knows the customer and that they may use the number. Attestation B means the customer is known and the number is not verified. Attestation C is a gateway call the provider did not authenticate. The Pallone-Thune TRACED Act is Public Law 116-105 (2019). Shifting FCC deadline dates are omitted.

    Calls arrive with no verification

    Checks. Is the inbound INVITE missing Identity, or is verification failing?

    Likely cause. The originating provider did not sign, or the certificate chain failed.

    Fix. Capture the Identity header. Do not strip it on your SBC.

    Attestation is always C

    Checks. Is this traffic entering through a gateway the provider does not authenticate?

    Likely cause. Gateway attestation is what ATIS-1000074 calls C.

    Fix. C is a label, not a reason to block by itself. Policy is the provider's.

    Your SBC removes Identity

    Checks. Header manipulation on the trunk.

    Likely cause. A normalization rule treats Identity as unknown and drops it.

    Fix. Pass Identity and the required date and certificate fields untouched.
