Group 3 fax on the wire is ITU-T T.30. T.38 carries that session over IP by relaying the fax frames instead of hoping the tones survive RTP. G.711 pass-through leaves the tones in audio and breaks when there is loss, jitter, or a voice codec. RFC 3362 registers the SDP media type image/t38.

    Failure tree

    Fax works on a quiet LAN and fails on the WAN

    Checks. Loss and jitter on a G.711 pass-through call.

    Likely cause. T.30 cannot tolerate the loss that speech can hide.

    Fix. Switch to T.38, or reserve a clean G.711 path with no VAD.

    re-INVITE for image/t38 is rejected

    Checks. 488 or 415 on the re-INVITE.

    Likely cause. The far side has no T.38, or the SDP version is not agreed.

    Fix. Fall back to G.711 pass-through only if the path is clean. Otherwise fail the fax.

    Only the first page arrives

    Checks. High-speed training and error correction.

    Likely cause. A codec change or packet loss hit the page data.

    Fix. Keep a single media type for the whole session and capture both gateways.
