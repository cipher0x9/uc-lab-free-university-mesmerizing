RTP (RFC 3550) carries the speech. RTCP reports quality. SRTP (RFC 3711) encrypts RTP. RTCP-mux (RFC 5761) puts RTCP on the RTP port. DTMF prefers RFC 4733 telephone-events over in-band tones or SIP INFO. G.711 is 64 kbit/s at 8 kHz. G.729 is 8 kbit/s. G.722 is wideband at 16 kHz sampling. Opus is RFC 6716.

    Failure tree

    Audio chops every few seconds

    Checks. Jitter, loss, and whether the jitter buffer is growing. RTCP or RTCP XR (RFC 3611) if present.

    Likely cause. Loss or delay variation. Not a signaling failure if the ladder is clean.

    Fix. Fix the path. PLC hides short loss and does not repair a congested queue.

    Digits fail in IVR, voice is fine

    Checks. Is DTMF RFC 4733, in-band, or SIP INFO?

    Likely cause. The IVR expects telephone-events and the trunk is sending in-band tones, or the reverse.

    Fix. Match one method on both ends. Do not leave two methods on and hope.

    Fax or modem dies on a compressed codec

    Checks. Was the call G.711, or G.729/Opus?

    Likely cause. Low-bitrate speech codecs destroy modem tones.

    Fix. Use G.711 pass-through or T.38 for fax. See the fax page.
